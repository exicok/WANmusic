# Prism Pulse design contract

- Layout: native-preserving. Codex navigation, task geometry, composer, project selector, scrolling, menus, and state behavior remain native.
- Background scope: home artwork plus a subtle route-neutral workspace watermark. The task page never receives a full illustrative backdrop.
- Decoration density: balanced on home, minimal on tasks.
- Mode: light. A mist-white surface keeps long coding sessions readable while cyan, teal, magenta, and warm yellow supply the electronic-music energy.
- Artwork focal point: an original translucent synthesizer crystal and orbiting rhythm rings on the right. The left 48% is quiet and text-safe. No character, costume, hairstyle, logo, signature, or branded silhouette.
- Semantic palette: ink `#18343B`, muted ink `#587078`, accent `#16BDB2`, deep accent `#087F80`, secondary `#E46EAC`, highlight `#FFD166`, surface `#F4FBFC`, sidebar `#16383E`.
- Allowed changes: colors, borders, shadows, background artwork, home hero crop, suggestion-card finish, input surface, and sidebar selection finish.
- Preserved states: native hit areas, hover-only clear-project control, focus rings, caret, composer actions, disclosure controls, usage card contrast, reduced motion, and horizontal scrolling behavior.
- Home art crop: height-fit, right-center, flush to the right edge; focal geometry stays inside a 10% top/right safe region.
- Workspace art crop: a small, soft, noninteractive lower-right watermark with no hard bitmap edge.
- Verification viewports: current Windows desktop viewport and narrow layout at the built-in `900px` breakpoint where available.
